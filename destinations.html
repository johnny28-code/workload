Semonkong (maseru district) NATURE & ADVENTURE

Maletsunyane Falls

192m Single Drop World Record Abseil Pony Trekking

Plunging 192 metres (630 ft) in an unbroken single drop into a sheer basalt canyon near Semonkong ("Place of Smoke"). It holds the Guinness World Record for the highest commercial abseil (204m) and is a legendary pony trekking destination.

Thaba Bosiu Cultural Village

National Monument Founded 1824 Sacred Plateau

The historic mountain citadel where King Moshoeshoe I united the Basotho nation in 1824. Tour the royal tombs of Basotho monarchs, view the iconic Mount Qiloane, and experience living heritage at the modern Cultural Village.

Mokhotlong District MOUNTAINS & ADVENTURE

Sani Pass & Escarpment

2,876m Summit 4x4 Iconic Route Highest Pub in Africa

Southern Africa's most famous mountain pass, climbing 2,876 metres up sheer rocky hairpin bends. Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views from the escarpment and warm up at the legendary "Highest Pub in Africa" at Sani Top.

Indigenous Forest Berg Bamboo Maliba Mountain Lodge

Spanning 5,600 hectares in the northern Maloti Mountains, this reserve preserves Lesotho's rare sub-alpine indigenous trees, dense mountain bamboo groves, pristine swimming streams, and luxury eco-lodge hospitality.

Thaba-Tseka District ENGINEERING & SCENERY

Katse Dam & Botanical Garden

185m Arch Wall Alpine Reservoir Water Project

Africa's second-largest double-curvature arch dam wall stands 185 metres high on the Malibamat'so River. Visitors can tour inside the monumental dam wall galleries, embark on boat cruises, and explore the alpine Katse Botanical Garden.

AfriSki & Mahlasela Pass

3,222m Elevation Winter Ski Resort Summer Downhill MTB

Situated at 3,222 metres on the Mahlasela Pass, AfriSki is one of Africa's few winter ski and snowboard resorts (June–August). In summer, it hosts premier mountain biking, trail running, and high-altitude endurance training.

Ha Kome Cave Dwellings

19th-Century Caves Living Heritage Berea District

Unique 19th-century mud-and-thatch cave houses built beneath a towering curved sandstone overhang. Established during the Lifaqane wars as a mountain sanctuary, they remain occupied today by descendants of the original Kome clan.
